# Composition reference

## Subject-safe overlay zone

For a 1920×1080 background, treat approximately x=620–1300 and y=170–1040 as the primary webcam-subject zone. This is a planning guide, not a hard mask.

Keep the brightest skyline features and narrow vertical window mullions outside the central face region when possible.

## Preferred room geometry

- Camera: opposite side of office, seated/standing webcam height, facing the chair.
- Chair: centered or within ~5% of center.
- Desk: behind chair, generally against the window wall or rear wall.
- Windows: broad enough to establish the city without turning the background into a skyline poster.
- Floor: visible around the chair to make the room read as real.
- Foreground: open.

## Export

Preferred:
- 1920×1080 JPG for broad compatibility.
- PNG when the source contains fine line detail and file size is acceptable.
- Keep compression high enough that skyline edges and chair mesh do not turn blocky.

## Geographic realism

For real cities, use a small number of recognizable cues instead of forcing every landmark into one view. It is better to create a plausible office orientation with two or three accurate cues than an impossible panorama.
